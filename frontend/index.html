        What is it?

          What to look for

          Something that hops

          Stars far away stay put. A nearby star, brown dwarf or asteroid shifts between passes. In Difference view it leaves a dark spot where it was and a bright one where it went.

          Something red

          In Colour view ordinary stars are white. Red means bright only at the longest wavelengths, the mark of a cold object such as a brown dwarf or a distant planet.

          Something that flickers

          A star that brightens or fades between passes while its neighbours hold steady is variable. Click it to see how much, and at which wavelengths.

          Is it already known?

          Circles mark Gaia stars, diamonds mark other infrared catalogue sources, and names come from SIMBAD. Click any dot to see everything four catalogues list there. "Nothing catalogued" means check further, not that it is new.
